06 / ENGINEERING NOTES

Know what the model assumes.

01

Short circuit

Fixed pack open-circuit voltage and cell DCIR. Adiabatic conductor heating at resistivity corrected to the hot-start temperature. Cell voltage collapse and temperature-dependent resistance during the fault are not modelled.

02

Continuous current

Lumped temperature response per unit length: heat input I²ρ/A, heat loss hPΔT and time constant density·A·Cp/(hP). No axial conduction or weld heating; all four conductor faces are assumed exposed.

03

Design review

The workbook labels many inputs as provisional. Verify CAD dimensions, cell DCIR across state of charge and temperature, clearing-time and I²t characteristics, real heat transfer, plating and adjacent component limits before using results for a design decision.
